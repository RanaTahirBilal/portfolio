Neat Roots Inc. Mutual NDA

Mutual Confidentiality and Non Disclosure Agreement

Please read in full. The signature section is at the foot of the page.

This Mutual Confidentiality and Non Disclosure Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into between:

Neat Roots Inc., a corporation (the “Service Provider”)

and

the client identified in the signature section below, operating under the brand name La Patrona (the “Client”).

Effective Date:

Each of the Service Provider and the Client may be referred to as a “Party,” and together as the “Parties.”

In the course of their professional relationship, the Parties expect to share information that is private, commercially sensitive, or otherwise not publicly available. This Agreement sets out how that information is to be handled. It is intended to be fair, mutual, and workable for both Parties, and to support a relationship built on professionalism and trust.

1. Purpose

The Parties may exchange Confidential Information in connection with their business and professional relationship, which may include social media management, Instagram and YouTube operations, content publishing and scheduling, analytics and reporting, brand collaborations, advertising activities, commercial discussions, and related professional services.

Each Party agrees to protect Confidential Information received from the other Party and to use it only for purposes connected with that relationship.

This Agreement applies to Confidential Information exchanged between the Parties before, on, and after the Effective Date, including any account credentials or access information shared before this Agreement was signed.

2. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means information disclosed by one Party (the “Disclosing Party”) to the other (the “Receiving Party”) that is not publicly available and that a reasonable person would understand to be private, commercially sensitive, or confidential, whether or not it is marked as such.

Depending on the circumstances, Confidential Information may include:

  • Instagram account information
  • YouTube account information
  • Account credentials and login information
  • Security and authentication information
  • Account analytics and performance data
  • Private messages and direct messages
  • Unpublished content, photographs, and videos
  • Content calendars and content plans
  • Publishing schedules
  • Social media strategies and recommendations
  • Business processes and internal systems
  • Templates and workflows
  • Brand contacts and brand opportunities
  • Brand negotiations and proposals
  • Campaign information and campaign performance
  • Contracts and compensation information
  • Financial information and pricing
  • Business plans and commercial information
  • Client lists and business relationships
  • Advertising information
  • Private communications between the Parties
  • Screenshots and recordings
  • Personal information shared for the relationship
  • Other nonpublic information disclosed in connection with the relationship

Confidential Information may be communicated verbally, electronically, visually, in writing, through shared files, through access to accounts or systems, or by any other reasonable means.

3. Permitted Use

The Receiving Party will use the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information only as reasonably necessary to perform, manage, or support the professional relationship between the Parties.

The Receiving Party will not use Confidential Information for an unrelated personal, commercial, or competitive purpose, and will not disclose it to a third party, except as permitted by this Agreement or as authorized by the Disclosing Party.

The Receiving Party may share Confidential Information with its own personnel or professional advisers who need it for the purposes of the relationship, provided they are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement, and the Receiving Party remains responsible for their handling of it.

4. Account Ownership and Security

Ownership

The Client is and remains the owner of the Client’s Instagram account, YouTube account, and any other social media or online accounts operated under the Client’s brand. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of, or any right in, those accounts to the Service Provider.

Access

The Service Provider will receive only the access reasonably necessary to perform the agreed services. Any credentials, login details, authentication information, or other access information provided to the Service Provider are Confidential Information and will be used only to perform those services.

Safeguards

Each Party will use reasonable safeguards to protect the other Party’s Confidential Information, using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than reasonable care.

Neither Party will knowingly provide the other Party’s credentials or access information to an unauthorized third party.

Security incidents

If a Party becomes aware of a material unauthorized access to, or a security incident affecting, the other Party’s Confidential Information, it will notify the other Party without undue delay and to the extent reasonably appropriate in the circumstances, and will cooperate reasonably in addressing it.

The Client likewise agrees to keep confidential any account access information, internal systems, processes, or business information belonging to the Service Provider.

5. Brand and Commercial Information

Information concerning brand relationships and brand collaborations is Confidential Information unless it has already been made public by the relevant Party or by the brand concerned.

This includes proposed and ongoing collaborations, negotiations, proposals, campaign requirements, campaign performance, contact information for brand representatives, contracts, compensation, and other commercially sensitive details.

This provision protects both Parties. The Client’s commercial arrangements stay private, and the Service Provider’s commercial relationships and working methods stay private.

6. Permitted Disclosure

A Party may disclose Confidential Information where disclosure is reasonably necessary to:

  1. comply with applicable law;
  2. comply with a valid subpoena, court order, regulatory requirement, or other binding governmental request; or
  3. obtain professional legal, accounting, tax, or similar advice, provided the adviser is subject to a duty of confidentiality.

Where a Party is compelled to disclose Confidential Information, it will, to the extent legally permitted and reasonably practicable, give the other Party prompt notice before disclosing, so that the other Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate relief. The Party will disclose only the portion of Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

Nothing in this Section requires a Party to act in a way that would conflict with applicable law.

7. Exclusions

Information is not Confidential Information, and the obligations in this Agreement do not apply to it, where the Receiving Party can show that the information:

  1. is or becomes publicly available through no act or omission of the Receiving Party in breach of this Agreement;
  2. was lawfully known to the Receiving Party, without a confidentiality obligation, before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party;
  3. is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information; or
  4. is lawfully received from a third party that was entitled to disclose it and did not impose a confidentiality obligation.

8. No Transfer of Ownership

Disclosure of Confidential Information under this Agreement does not transfer any ownership, intellectual property right, licence, or other right in that information. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party, except where the Parties agree otherwise in a separate written agreement.

This Agreement does not grant either Party a right to use the other Party’s name, trade marks, logos, or content, except as separately agreed in writing.

9. Return or Deletion of Confidential Information

On the reasonable written request of the Disclosing Party, or when the professional relationship ends, the Receiving Party will return or delete the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information where it is reasonably practicable to do so, and will discontinue further use of it.

A Party may retain Confidential Information to the extent reasonably required for legal, accounting, compliance, insurance, backup, or ordinary recordkeeping purposes, or where deletion is not technically practicable. Information retained on that basis remains subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement for as long as it is retained.

Where the Service Provider holds access to the Client’s accounts, that access will be removed or relinquished promptly on request or at the end of the relationship.

10. Confidential Relationship Information

Neither Party will publicly disclose the other Party’s Confidential Information, including private business communications, account information, confidential negotiations, campaign or compensation details, or other confidential matters concerning the relationship, without the other Party’s authorization.

This Section applies only to Confidential Information. It does not prevent either Party from discussing the relationship generally, from describing the services provided or received, from making truthful statements, or from exercising any right described in Section 13.

11. Mutual Obligation

This Agreement is mutual. The Client has an obligation to protect Confidential Information belonging to the Service Provider, and the Service Provider has an obligation to protect Confidential Information belonging to the Client. The Parties intend these obligations to apply equally and in the same way to each of them.

12. Duration

This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for as long as the Parties maintain a professional relationship.

The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement continue as follows:

  1. Ordinary Confidential Information. Protected for a period of three (3) years following the end of the professional relationship.
  2. Trade secrets. Information that qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law remains protected for as long as it continues to qualify as a trade secret.
  3. Account credentials and security information. Protected for as long as the relevant credentials or security information remain in use, and in any event for the period in paragraph 1.

A fixed three year period is used for ordinary information so that the obligation is clear and proportionate, rather than indefinite. Trade secrets are treated separately because the law already protects them for as long as they remain secret.

13. Protected Legal Rights

Nothing in this Agreement prevents, limits, or penalizes either Party from:

  1. making a disclosure that is protected or required by applicable law;
  2. reporting suspected unlawful conduct to a government agency or law enforcement authority, or participating in an investigation or proceeding conducted by such an authority;
  3. cooperating with a government authority where legally required; or
  4. exercising any right that cannot lawfully be waived or restricted by agreement.

A Party is not required to give the other Party notice before making a disclosure of the kind described in this Section.

14. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause harm for which monetary damages alone may not be an adequate remedy.

Accordingly, and to the extent permitted by applicable law, a Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy available to it at law or in equity, without this limiting any other right or remedy.

This Agreement does not provide for liquidated damages, penalties, or automatic financial consequences. Any remedy is a matter for determination under applicable law.

15. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

16. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, unlawful, or unenforceable, that provision will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or, if it cannot be modified, severed. The remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

17. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire understanding between the Parties regarding the confidentiality and protection of information exchanged in connection with their professional relationship, and supersedes any earlier understanding on that subject.

This Agreement supplements, and does not replace, any separate services, management, or commercial agreement between the Parties. It does not set out, modify, or affect fees, payment terms, service scope, duration of services, termination rights, revenue arrangements, or any other commercial term, all of which are governed by that separate agreement.

If there is a conflict between this Agreement and the confidentiality provisions of a separate agreement, the provision giving the greater lawful protection to Confidential Information applies, to the extent permitted by law.

18. Amendments

Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and agreed to by both Parties. An amendment agreed by electronic means, including by email or electronic signature, is effective to the extent permitted by applicable law.

19. Electronic Signatures and Counterparts

This Agreement may be signed electronically, and an electronic signature has the same effect as a handwritten signature to the extent permitted by applicable law.

This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together form one agreement. A scanned, photographed, or electronically transmitted copy of a signature has the same effect as an original.

20. Acknowledgment

Each Party confirms that it has read this Agreement, understands its purpose, has had the opportunity to seek independent professional advice, and enters into it voluntarily.

The Parties intend this Agreement to support a professional relationship built on trust, transparency, and mutual respect, and to handle each other’s confidential information responsibly.
