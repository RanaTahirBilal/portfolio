About

Rana Tahir Bilal

Agentic AI engineer. I build systems that take over the work you repeat, and I make accomplished people findable and correctly described, on Google and inside the assistants their clients now ask first.

What I do now

Two things, and they are the same thing seen from different sides.

I build agentic AI systems that take over work a person or a small team repeats every week. Not a demo. Something that runs on a Tuesday, fails in ways you have already decided are acceptable, and tells you when it is unsure.

And I build the record that makes accomplished people findable and correctly described, on Google and inside the AI assistants their clients now ask first. A site under their own name, structured so a search engine can read it and a language model can quote it without inventing the parts it cannot find.

Where the engineering came from

Eight years building Android applications that shipped to real users. Kotlin, Java, Firebase, MVVM, CI pipelines, crash triage. The unglamorous half of that work turned out to be the half that matters in AI.

Mobile teaches you to assume the network fails, to design for the slow failure as well as the fast one, and to rank a wall of crashes by who actually got hurt. Most agents being built today have none of that. They call a tool, assume it returns, and assume what returns is valid. Three assumptions, all of them wrong eventually.

That is the whole of my advantage and I lead with it.

I am an entrepreneur, not a contractor

I founded Neat Roots in 2020 and built the channel from zero to 330,000 subscribers. I did the positioning, the content, the ads, the funnel and the team. That is the same job I now do for other people, run on my own name first, where every number is public and nobody could cover for me if it failed.

Building audiences is a discipline, not a talent. What ranks, what gets clicked, what gets trusted and what quietly gets ignored: I learned all of it against a live audience that told me within a day when I got it wrong.

Since then I have worked with physicians, attorneys, scientists, professors, a college president and executives. See the work.

What I will not do

I do not manufacture authority. If your record does not support a claim, I will not write the claim, and I will say so before you pay me rather than after.

I do not promise a position on Google. Nobody can. What I can promise is that the page is correct, fast, structured properly, and says what you actually do, which is the only part of ranking anyone controls.

I work as one person, not an agency. You deal with me.
