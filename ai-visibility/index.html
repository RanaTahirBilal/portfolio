AI visibility

What does ChatGPT say when someone asks about you?

Answer engine and generative engine optimisation for people whose reputation arrives before they do. The goal is not a ranking. It is an accurate answer.

The part most people have never checked

Open ChatGPT and ask it about yourself by name. Then ask Claude, then Gemini. Most accomplished people have never done this, and the answer is usually one of three things.

  • It does not know who you are. You get a polite refusal, or a generic answer about someone with a similar name.
  • It knows an older version of you. A job you left, a title you outgrew, a company that no longer exists.
  • It hedges. It describes you but attributes everything to you, because nothing independent confirms it.

The third one is the most damaging and the least obvious. A model that cannot verify a claim will say so, and "according to his own website" reads very differently from a plain statement of fact.

Why this is not SEO

Search ranks pages. A language model answers a question, usually without showing you a list, and increasingly that answer is the only thing a person sees. Being on page one is no longer the same as being the answer.

The mechanics are different too. Search rewards links and authority. Models reward being legible: a clear entity, described consistently in the places a crawler trusts, with the same name, the same role and the same facts everywhere it looks. Contradictions are what make a model hedge.

What the work actually is

Entity consolidation

One canonical description of you, reconciled across your site, LinkedIn, professional directories, and anywhere else you appear. Models compare sources. Disagreement costs you.

Structured data

Machine readable markup that states who you are, what you do, and which profiles are yours, so a crawler does not have to guess from prose.

Source material

Pages written to be quoted: specific, checkable, and organised so an assistant can lift an accurate paragraph instead of assembling one.

Crawler access

The AI crawlers are explicitly allowed, the sitemap is submitted, and the pages are indexed. Plenty of sites quietly block the crawlers they most want.

A measured starting point

I record what each assistant says about you before I touch anything, so there is something honest to compare against later.

Re-checking

Models retrain and re-crawl on their own schedule. Change is not instant and I will not pretend otherwise. I check again rather than declare victory.

What I will not claim

I cannot make a model say a particular sentence about you. Nobody can, and anyone selling that is selling something they do not control.

What is controllable is the input: whether the facts exist in a form a machine can read, whether they agree with each other, and whether the crawlers are allowed in. That is the work. The rest is the model's decision.
