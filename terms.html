The one thing worth knowing up front

Your engagement starts the day you pay. No queue, no waiting list. Research, writing and design begin the same day, which is why the fee is payable in full and is non refundable once that work has started. Section 9 sets this out plainly, and if you would rather begin later, just email info@ranatahirbilal.com and your place is held until you are ready.

3What I need from you

The engagement moves at the speed of your input, so a little preparation goes a long way. You agree to:

  • provide biography details, career history, photographs and any other requested material
  • respond to questions and review requests within seven calendar days
  • give a single named point of contact for approvals
  • make sure you hold the rights to every photograph, logo, text and document you supply

If I do not hear from you for sixty consecutive days, the engagement is treated as paused at your request. Work already carried out remains chargeable. Your file is held for twelve months and work resumes whenever you are ready.

4When the work begins

Work begins on the day payment is received. In practice that means research into your background and record, planning the structure of the site, and the first drafting and design work, all on the day you pay.

"Work has commenced" means any of the following has happened: research or preparation has been carried out, a questionnaire or intake document has been sent to you, drafting or design has started, or a domain, hosting or third party service has been configured for you.

5Timeline and delivery

Indicative build time is four to eight weeks from receipt of your material, depending on the engagement.

Where an instalment plan is in place, the finished site is launched on your domain in the final month of the plan. Drafts, previews and progress are shared throughout, so you see the work developing rather than waiting in the dark.

Delays caused by late or incomplete material extend the timeline accordingly.

6Revisions and putting things right

Each engagement includes two full rounds of revisions on written content and design before launch. A round means one consolidated set of comments rather than a stream of individual requests.

If something delivered does not match what was agreed, tell me in writing and I will correct it at no charge. Please allow ten business days to put it right before treating the matter as unresolved. Most concerns turn out to be a misunderstanding about scope and are settled in a single email.

Further revisions, or changes to scope agreed after work has started, are quoted separately and charged additionally.

7Ownership of the work

On receipt of the full fee, ownership of the final delivered website transfers to you. That covers the written content, the page designs as delivered, and images created specifically for you. You may use, host, alter and move it freely.

Until the full fee is paid, the work remains my property and is licensed to you for review only.

I keep ownership of the underlying tools, code libraries, templates, frameworks and working methods used to produce it. These are not sold to you and are reused across engagements, so you are not buying exclusivity in how the work is made.

Assets from third parties, such as fonts, stock images and plugins, remain with their licensors and pass to you under their own licences.

8Payment

Payment runs through Stripe. I never see, handle or store your card details.

The fee for an engagement is payable in full. An instalment plan is a way of spreading that same fee across an agreed number of months. It is a payment schedule, not a reduction, and it is not a subscription.

Paying in full

The whole fee is charged once, at purchase. On Digital Authority this is the discounted price.

Paying by instalments

Where you choose an instalment plan, you authorise a recurring charge to your card of the stated amount, once per month, for the stated number of payments. The first payment is taken on the day you purchase, and the rest follow on the same date of each month after that.

If an instalment fails, I will contact you and Stripe will retry. If payment is not brought up to date within fourteen days, work pauses and any launch is postponed until the balance is settled. The outstanding balance remains payable.

How an instalment plan ends

An instalment plan runs for a fixed number of payments and then stops. Professional Presence has no plan. Executive Authority is three payments. Digital Authority is four. There is no renewal, and no payment is taken after the final one.

You can stop an instalment plan at any time, and it is meant to be easy. There are two ways, and either one works on its own. You can cancel yourself at any hour through the billing portal, where you enter your email, receive a link, and press cancel. Or you can email info@ranatahirbilal.com with the word cancel and your plan is stopped, with confirmation sent to you within one business day. You never have to call anyone or explain yourself.

Within one business day of your first payment you will receive written confirmation setting out the amount of each payment, how many payments there are, the date of each one, the total, and how to cancel. Keep that email. It is your record of the plan.

9Refunds and cancellation

Refund policy

Before work has commenced: a full refund, less any third party costs already incurred on your behalf.

After work has commenced: fees paid are non refundable, because the work they paid for has already been carried out.

If you cancel mid engagement: no further instalments are charged, and everything produced up to that point is yours to keep. Sums already paid are not returned.

This policy is shown to you before purchase and accepted at checkout, so there are no surprises later.

Where a refund is due, it is issued to the original payment method within ten business days of being agreed. Your bank may take a few days more to show it.

Nothing in this section limits a right you hold under law that cannot be set aside by agreement, and nothing here removes a remedy where I have failed to deliver what was agreed. Section 6 sets out how anything wrong gets fixed.

Part twoPrivacy

This part explains what happens to your information. It applies to this website and to every engagement, and it forms part of the agreement above.

18Your choices, and how to use them

You stay in control of your own information. At any time you can ask me to:

  • tell you exactly what I hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your material, where no accounting or legal obligation requires me to keep it
  • send you a copy of everything you provided

Email info@ranatahirbilal.com and you will have a reply within thirty days, usually much sooner. There is no charge and no catch, and asking changes nothing about the service you receive.

Depending on where you live you may hold further rights, for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR. Those are honoured on request, wherever you are.

19Nothing is sold, and nothing is tracked

Personal information is not sold, and is not shared for cross context behavioural advertising. There is no advertising relationship of any kind behind this site.

Because the site does no tracking in the first place, a Do Not Track signal has nothing to switch off. You are already as private here as that setting could make you.

20Keeping things safe

The site is served over HTTPS. Card details never touch my systems at any point. The accounts that hold your information are protected with strong, unique passwords and two step authentication.

No system anywhere is perfectly secure, and I will not pretend otherwise. If a breach ever affected your information, you would hear it from me, promptly, along with the authorities the law requires me to notify.

21How long anything is kept

  • Email correspondence. Kept while we are in contact and for a reasonable period afterwards, in case a question comes up later.
  • Payment and engagement records. Kept for seven years, which is what tax and accounting obligations require.
  • Working material you sent. Kept for twelve months after launch so revisions stay possible, then deleted whenever you ask.

22Children

This is a service for working professionals. It is not aimed at children, and information about anyone under sixteen is not knowingly collected. If you believe a child's information has reached me, write to me and it will be deleted.

23Where your information is handled

The practice is based in the United States, and the services listed below operate principally from the United States. If you write or buy from outside the US, your information is handled there.

24What happens when you simply visit

The pages are static files. Nothing is stored on your device, and no profile of you is built.

Hosting records

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records standard request information such as IP address, browser type, page requested and time, in order to deliver pages and guard against abuse. I do not receive those logs, query them, or keep them.

Fonts

Typefaces load from Google Fonts. To send the font files, Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google states that Google Fonts requests are not used to build advertising profiles. No cookie is set by this.

Links out

Some pages link to LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, Coursera and elsewhere. Once you follow a link you are on someone else's site, under their privacy policy rather than this one.

25What happens when you email

Email is the only way to reach me from this site, through plain mailto: links. When you write to info@ranatahirbilal.com or support@ranatahirbilal.com, your message is routed by Namecheap's email forwarding into a Google hosted inbox that I read.

I keep what you send, meaning your name, your email address and whatever you chose to write, so that I can answer you and keep a record of our correspondence. You are not added to a mailing list, and no marketing email is ever sent.

26Material you share during an engagement

Building a personal brand website means you send me material about yourself: biography, career history, credentials and qualifications, photographs, published work, and sometimes a CV.

That material is used only to research, write, design and build your website, and to publish the parts you approve. It is used for nothing else, and goes no further than the services listed in Section 12.

If your material mentions other people, such as colleagues, co authors, patients or clients, you are responsible for having the right to share it. Please take out anything confidential before sending.
