Selected work

Thirty one people who were easier to respect than to find.

Personal brand websites for professionals with a real record and no place online that shows it.

Every one of them was losing work quietly

Not one of these people had a visibility problem they could see. They had decades of record, full diaries and good reputations inside their own field. What they did not have was a page that said so, under their own name, to somebody checking them for the first time.

So the checking happened anyway, against whatever was there: a directory profile they could not edit, a practice page written by a marketing team, a stale bio from a conference in 2014. Some of the work went elsewhere and nobody ever told them.

That is the entire job. Put the record where the checking happens, in their words, and stop the leak.

Sixteen of the thirty one

Physicians, attorneys, academics, scientists and executives. Every one of these is a live site built under the client's own name. The full set is on the home page.

What gets built

A site under your own name

Your domain, your record, not a profile on a platform that can change its rules or disappear.

The biography done properly

Written from what you have actually done, in language a person outside your field can follow.

Structured for machines

Markup that tells search engines and AI assistants who you are, so they stop guessing.

Fast and legible on a phone

Most people who search your name are holding a phone. That is the version that has to work.
