How it works

Three steps
01

The brief

You answer a questionnaire about your work, your record and what you would rather not publish. It takes about forty minutes.

02

The build

I write and design the site from your answers. Nothing is invented: no awards, dates, quotes or history that you have not confirmed.

03

Live

Hosted, fast, readable on a phone, and yours. You get the files and the address, and I stay on hand for changes.

If your work is hard to explain, that is the job.
