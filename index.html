What happens when someone Googles your name?

Most people will search you before they meet you. What they find is the version of you they act on.

  • 01An investor, before a term sheet
  • 02A journalist, before a quote
  • 03A conference organiser, before an invitation
  • 04A prospective partner, before a first meeting
  • 05A high value client, before a referral is accepted

Your reputation offline may be settled. Online it is usually fragmented: a profile you did not write, a company bio that will change when you leave, a directory entry, an old interview, a handful of third party pages you do not control.

None of them is wrong. Together they are simply incoherent, and the person searching has to assemble you from pieces.

I do not manufacture authority.

I do not inflate credentials, and I do not turn ordinary accomplishments into marketing fiction. No award, date, title, quote, publication or employer appears on a site unless the person has confirmed it. If a detail cannot be verified it is marked for review, not filled in.

That restraint is the point. The people I work with have spent decades building something real, and the fastest way to damage it is to overstate it in public.

My job is to take the work, expertise and reputation you have already built and construct the digital infrastructure that represents it properly.

Before I build anything.

The first conversation is not about a website. It is about what someone should see when they search for you, and what currently stands between them and that.

I have built an audience of my own: 330,000 subscribers and 434 videos on YouTube at Neat Roots, teaching developers to build applications, and around 24,800 people on Instagram. Useful evidence that I understand how attention and reputation travel online, and not the reason to hire me.

About

An AI engineer who builds personal brand websites.

I work with founders, executives, clinicians, attorneys and public figures who have spent decades building something real, and have almost nothing online that reflects it. I build the site, the structure and the digital presence that represents the record accurately.

Before I did this for anyone else, I did it for myself. I built an audience from zero to 330,000 subscribers on YouTube and 24,800 on Instagram, teaching people to build software. No agency, no budget, no shortcut. I know what makes a stranger stop on a name, decide it is worth trusting, and come back, because I had to learn it in public and I am still doing it.

The marketing is trained, not improvised. I studied Marketing in a Digital World and Digital Marketing and Customer Engagement Strategy at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Positioning a person is not something I picked up beside the code. It is half of what I do.

The other half is engineering. Six years writing Android line by line, certified as an Android developer, then AI engineering: agentic systems, LLM applications and AI agents built with Claude, with Anthropic certification behind the work.

That is why these sites are built the way they are. Every claim is checked before it is published. Every page is tested at real screen sizes and against real accessibility thresholds, not eyeballed. Nothing ships because it looks finished.
