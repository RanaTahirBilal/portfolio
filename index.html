I do not invent things.

No award, date, title, quote, publication or employer goes on a site unless the person confirmed it to me in writing. If a detail cannot be verified, it is marked for review, not filled in.

That sounds like a small promise. It is the reason these sites hold up when a client, a regulator or a journalist goes looking. It is also why the work starts with questions rather than a template.

Where a site needs a photograph that no camera took, the person says yes to that in writing first, and the page says so in its footer.

Three steps, start to live.

Step one

The brief

You answer a questionnaire about your career, your record, and what you would rather not publish. Roughly forty minutes, in as many sittings as you like.

Step two

The build

I write and design the site from your answers. You see it before anyone else does, and nothing goes live until you have read every line of it.

Step three

Live

Hosted, fast, readable on a phone, and yours. You get the files and the address, and I stay on hand when something needs changing.

If your work is hard to explain, that is the job.

Tell me what you have done and I will tell you honestly whether a site will help. The first conversation costs nothing.
